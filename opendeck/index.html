OpenDeck DJ

A single-deck DJ player for iPad that plays your tracks and syncs to Pioneer CDJ/XDJ players over Pro DJ Link — turning a tablet into a drop-in extra deck on the network.

iOS / iPadOS

What it is

OpenDeck DJ is a faithful single-deck player modeled on a professional CDJ/XDJ layout: a jog wheel with a spinning platter display, a tempo fader, transport controls, waveform overview, and beat-grid analysis. It speaks Pioneer Pro DJ Link, so it appears on a DJ Link network alongside real players and can follow — or lead — the shared tempo and beat grid.

  • Load and play a track with an XDJ-style deck interface
  • Automatic BPM detection and beat-grid analysis
  • Jog-wheel scratching, nudging, and cueing
  • Pro DJ Link sync with CDJ/XDJ players on your local network
  • Runs entirely on-device — no account, no sign-in

Privacy

OpenDeck DJ does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Local-network access is used only to sync with Pioneer CDJ/XDJ players over Pro DJ Link, and nothing leaves your device or network.
